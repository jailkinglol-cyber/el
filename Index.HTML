5 Critical Retribution Factors

1. Skill + Retri Combo

Combining heavy damage skills (e.g., Balmond Ult, Lancelot S2) simultaneously with Retribution instantly elevates your secure threshold way beyond pure Retri true damage.

2. Latency & Ping Delay

A higher ping creates a server processing gap. If your ping is 80ms, you must cast your Retribution ~0.1 seconds earlier than the calculated HP threshold.

3. Enemy DPS Burst Rate

When multiple enemy heroes hit the Lord together, objective HP drops erratically. Anticipate HP decay speeds rather than reacting visually.

4. True Damage Scaling

Retribution deals True Damage based on Jungler level. Always check level leads before contesting objectives to know your upper hand.
